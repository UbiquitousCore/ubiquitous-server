# Eristar Temple (Young Deity's Realm) — Quest Guide

*Player-style guide. Everything below is something you would see on screen: quest text, rewards, objectives. Money is converted to the coins you will see in game (1 gold = 100 silver = 10,000 copper).*

**Dungeon:** Eristar Temple, called **Young Deity's Realm** in quest text · enter from level 30 · up to 4 players
**Bosses:** Crystal Guard, the Four Champions (Barstikar, Ansurui, Ultanjin, Gindular) and Khaphichelur, the dragon. See the [Dungeon Field Guide](dungeon-field-guide.md#eristar-temple) for the fights.

I found no Normal-mode quests for Eristar Temple and no quest-givers inside it. Its quests are two level-60 daily bounties from the Eristars of the **Aryurumoka camp** in High Forest (past Greenmont, on the road to the southeast). Both can only be completed in **Legend mode**.

## Wanted by Aryurumoka: Khaphichelur (Daily)

- **Start / hand-in:** Arzarany at the Aryurumoka camp.
- **Story:** The Eristars say they are *"the steadfast enemies of evil"* who collect soul signets from the ground and use the souls of fallen foes to empower their people. Today they want you to *"cross the void and enter Young Deity's Realm and find Khaphichelur, the corrupt Region Guard,"* and kill him.
- **Objective:** Kill **Khaphichelur** in Young Deity's Realm. The quest says **you must complete it in Legend mode**.
- **Hand-in text:** *"You honor us with your deed."*
- **Reward:** **Essence of Soul Signet**, plus 24,000 XP and **4 gold 33 silver**.

## Summons For Help (Daily)

- **Start / hand-in:** Kristna at the Aryurumoka camp, who reads you an emergency message: Eristar Rock Summoners and Water Summoners have been *"causing havoc inside Young Deity's Realm with their elemental powers."*
- **Objective:** Kill **5 Eristar Rock Summoners** and **5 Eristar Water Summoners**. The quest says **you must complete it in Legend mode**, so only the Legend versions count.
- **Hand-in text:** *"What a relief! I'm not sure what would've happened if those Eristar Summoners got out of Young Deity's Realm!"*
- **Reward:** **Core of Soul Signet**, plus 24,000 XP and **4 gold 33 silver**.

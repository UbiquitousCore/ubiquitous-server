# Eidolon's Horizon — Quest Guide

*Player-style guide. Everything below is something you would see on screen: quest text, NPC dialogue, system messages, rewards. Money is converted to the coins you will see in game (1 gold = 100 silver = 10,000 copper).*

**Dungeon:** Eidolon's Horizon · enter from level 50 · up to 4 players
**Bosses:** Healique, the Crystal trials, Wethvive and Nacher; in Legend mode only, Lamiyt. See the [Dungeon Field Guide](dungeon-field-guide.md#eidolons-horizon) for the fights.

Eidolon's Horizon has no Normal-mode quests that I could find. Everything here is aimed at **Legend mode** and starts outside the dungeon:
- **Arzarany** and **Kristna** at the **Aryurumoka camp** in High Forest (past Greenmont, on the road to the southeast) give the daily bounties.
- **Africtany** (a Fire Elemental on a small island in Sinskaald Rift, Middle Land) and **Sabsker** ("the Speaker of the Elementals", in Storm Wind/Greenmont) run the *Lava Sigil*, *Blessing* and *A Sultan's Lament* chain.

The dailies say outright that they are **Legend mode** only. The sigil chain also revolves around Legend-mode creatures: Nacher's Legend form drops the sigils, and Lamiyt appears only in Legend mode.

## Wanted by Aryurumoka: Nacher (Daily)

- **Start / hand-in:** Arzarany.
- **Story:** *"There is a wizard gone mad, Nacher the Watcher."* He has built a dream world where victims think they've reached a utopia, then it turns into a nightmare. *"You must enter his dream world, and destroy him so that the victims of his nightmare can be set free."*
- **Objective:** Kill **Nacher** in Eidolon's Horizon. **Legend mode only.**
- **Hand-in text:** *"One day, Nacher must pay for the pain and death he inflicted on the victims lost in his nightmare."*
- **Reward:** **Essence of Soul Signet**, plus 24,000 XP and **4 gold 33 silver**.

## Victim of Nightmare (Daily)

- **Start / hand-in:** Kristna.
- **Story:** Nacher's minions still roam. People who sought dreams are now *"mindless automatons that only wish to do harm to others"*. In death they will find peace.
- **Objective:** Kill **20 Nacher's Servants**. The quest explains these are **Spirits of Nightmare** and **Residues of Nightmare**. **Legend mode only.**
- **Hand-in text:** *"They will feel free from so long a nightmare."*
- **Reward:** **Core of Soul Signet**, plus 24,000 XP and **4 gold 33 silver**.

## The Lava Sigil chain (weapon upgrades)

This is a three-step chain, done once per weapon type. There are **seven weapon types**: Staff, Shield, Halberd, Dagger (Knife), Bow (Longbow), Hammer and Relic.

### Step 1 — Get a Lava Sigil from Nacher

- **Nacher** has a very small chance (**0.05%**) to drop each of the seven sigils: **Lava Sigil: Staff, Shield, Halberd, Dagger, Bow, Hammer** and **Relic**.
- Each sigil is its own quest, named after it (*Lava Sigil: Staff*, etc.). There is no quest-giver: you hand the sigil in to **Africtany**.
- Africtany's flavour text: *"This little sigil comes from Nacher the Watcher's private collection. The sigil burns, and among the flames, you can see a shape - a giant standing under a fire-red sky... Perhaps a friendly Fire Elemental can help interpret its meaning."* When you speak to him he snaps *"You are no Fire Elemental! Tell me what you want, mortal!"*
- **Reward:** 24,000 XP and **4 gold 33 silver**. Handing it in unlocks **A Sultan's Lament** for the same weapon type, and you receive an **Activated Lava Sigil** when you accept that quest.

### Step 2 — Upgrade a Lava weapon (*Blessing from Africtany…*)

- **Start:** Sabsker. **Hand-in:** Africtany.
- Sabsker tells you he's sure you're the person Africtany is looking for, and says Africtany is *"waiting for you on a small island in Sinskaald Rift."* He adds that you can find Lava Cores *"in the dungeons of The Great Desert"*, Magical Blood Minerals in Sinskaald Rift, and for Flaming Hearts you *"need to ask the Aryurumoka."*
- Each quest takes a base Lava weapon plus materials and returns the upgraded weapon:

| You bring | Lava Cores | Magical Blood Minerals | Flaming Hearts | You get |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lava-Covered Halberd | 50 | 200 | 50 | Lava-Swallowed Halberd |
| Lava-Melted Staves | 50 | 200 | 50 | Lava-Rune Staves |
| Lava-Burned Longbow | 50 | 200 | 50 | Lava-Erupted Longbow |
| Lava-Broken Knife | 40 | 180 | 40 | Lava-Cohesion Knife |
| Lava-Tearing Shield | 40 | 180 | 40 | Lava-Solidified Shield |
| Lava-Blossomed Hammer | 35 | 165 | 35 | Lava-Crystallized Hammer |
| Lava-Blossomed Relic | 15 | 40 | 15 | Lava-Crystallized Relic |

- Each of these pays 12,000 XP and **2 gold 16 silver**.
- **Where things drop:**
  - **Lava Core:** Zargis (18%) and Nacher (20%) are the named sources.
  - **Magical Blood Mineral:** Crystal Guard, Rock General, Earth Spirit and Healique, among others.
  - **Flaming Heart:** I found no creature that drops it, so follow the quest's hint and ask the Aryurumoka.
  - **The base Lava weapons:** they drop at roughly 6–8% from special level-62 versions of **Khaphichelur, Kha-Anata, Ariel, Alr-Rahjil, Cosmo and Zargis**. These look like a seasonal (Christmas) event encounter in Middle Land rather than the normal dungeon bosses, but I could not confirm when they appear.

### Step 3 — A Sultan's Lament

- **Start:** Africtany. **Hand-in:** Sabsker. Requires the matching *Lava Sigil* quest from Step 1.
- **Story:** The sigil was made by **Incendor, the sultan of the Fire Elementals**. One of his Elementals escaped and was captured by Nacher, who used it to build his dream world. *"You must destroy the rogue Elemental and retrieve the burning core from inside it."* In exchange, Africtany says you will receive *"a weapon of great power"*: *"Bring a weapon to me, and I will imbue it with my powers."*
- **Objective:** Use the **Activated Lava Sigil** on a **Wethvive** in Eidolon's Horizon to summon **Lamiyt, Sultan's Betrayed Avatar**, and loot a **Spirit Shard** from its body. Then bring the Spirit Shard to Sabsker, together with the upgraded weapon for that quest.
- **Lamiyt:** level 62 with about 500,000 health. A Wethvive only turns into Lamiyt for a player who has one of the seven *A Sultan's Lament* quests active. After he falls you see **"The Teleportation Circle near the throne is shining."**, and Legend exhaustion points are added. He drops the Spirit Shard every time (100%).
- **Reward:** 24,000 XP and **4 gold 33 silver** for each of the seven versions. The quest text promises a "worthy reward", but I could not see an item attached beyond the XP and money, so I can't tell you what the weapon gets.

| Version | Needs the upgraded… |
|---|---|
| Lava Sigil: Staff → A Sultan's Lament | Lava-Rune Staves |
| Lava Sigil: Shield | Lava-Solidified Shield |
| Lava Sigil: Halberd | Lava-Swallowed Halberd |
| Lava Sigil: Dagger | Lava-Cohesion Knife |
| Lava Sigil: Bow | Lava-Erupted Longbow |
| Lava Sigil: Hammer | Lava-Crystallized Hammer |
| Lava Sigil: Relic | Lava-Crystallized Relic |

Every version also needs one **Spirit Shard**.

## Inside the dungeon

No friendly quest-givers stand inside Eidolon's Horizon. The dungeon does show a few messages worth knowing:
- **"Wake 25 Survivors in Deep Sleep to activate the guardian."**
- A character says **"Try both paths to see where they lead."**

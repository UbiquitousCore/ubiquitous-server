# Sailen — Quest & NPC Guide (The Lower City and The Palace)

*Player-style guide. Everything below is something you would see on screen: quest text, NPC dialogue, shouts, rewards. Money is converted to the coins you will see in game (1 gold = 100 silver = 10,000 copper).*

| Dungeon | Minimum level | Bosses (in order) |
|---|---|---|
| **Sailen: The Lower City** | 40 | Titehelm → Kha-Anata (plus extra Legend-only summons from the Ninth Gate) |
| **Sailen: The Palace** | 45 | Sacharist → Crethiyum → Zargis |

Up to 4 players in both. The Daisauros (Soumit, Penifet, Kuruma, Baweri) hand out the Normal-friendly quests in Middle Land. The Eristars of the Aryurumoka camp (Arzarany, Kristna, in High Forest) hand out the level-60 Legend dailies.

# The Lower City

## The Grudge
- **Start / hand-in:** Penifet (Middle Land).
- **Story:** A scholar's tale of the war between the Daisauros and the Saithions, and of **Kha-Anata**, the King of Sailen, who kept playing his lute as his kingdom fell. Travellers by the Lake of Souls come away with soured moods and nightmares.
- **Objective:** *"Kill Kha-Anata in Sailen: The Lower City, but first you should give Titehelm a good place to stay."* In practice that means **kill Titehelm first**. The dungeon makes Kha-Anata appear only after Titehelm has died.
- **Reward:** choose one of **Mirage Dagger**, **Mirage Battle Axe** or **Mirage Crossbow**, plus 30,000 XP and 3 gold 1 silver.

## Right a Wrong
- **Start / hand-in:** Kuruma (Middle Land). Sailen Priests kidnapped apprentices for sacrifices.
- **Objective:** Kill **5 Sailen Priests** in the Lower City.
- **Reward:** 30,000 XP and 3 gold 1 silver. No item.

## The Ninth Gate (Legend Mode)
- **Start / hand-in:** Baweri (Middle Land). Their dimensional gate was meant to summon heroes of the past but now calls out unpleasant creatures instead.
- **Objective:** Collect **4 fallen cards**: **Rock General's Card, Fire Origin's Card, Edison's Card and Golden Rooster's Card**. Each is a **guaranteed (100%) drop** from the matching creature. They only appear in Legend mode.
- **Reward:** choose **Change Wand (Evil Eye)** or **Change Wand (Dummy)**, plus 24,000 XP and 4 gold 33 silver.

## Wanted by Aryurumoka: Kha-Anata (Daily)
- **Start / hand-in:** Arzarany. *"He's in the middle of that lake, defiling a small island."*
- **Objective:** Kill Kha-Anata. **Legend mode only.**
- **Reward:** **Essence of Soul Signet**, plus 24,000 XP and 4 gold 33 silver.

## More Goblins, More Problems (Daily)
- **Start / hand-in:** Kristna. A notorious goblin slave chieftain and his slaves have camped deep in the Lower City.
- **Objective:** Kill the **Goblin Slave Chieftain** and **3 Goblin Slaves**. **Legend mode only.**
- **Reward:** **Core of Soul Signet**, plus 24,000 XP and 4 gold 33 silver.

## In the Lower City: the Ninth Gate (Legend only)

In **Legend mode only**, a friendly object called **The Ninth Gate** stands in the Lower City. Normal mode has no gate.

- Talk to it: *"It is not my willing to stand here; my mission is to summon heroes, not to prevent these strange creatures from passing. Is it your liking to summon them to this world?"* Choose **"Yes, it is my desire."**
- You get the system message **"You sense the enemy coming..."**, and a creature appears. It appears to work once **after Titehelm is dead** and once more **after Kha-Anata is dead**, so you can't call creatures out endlessly.
- What it summons is decided by a roll:

| Chance | Creature |
|---|---|
| 30% | **Rock General** |
| 30% | **Fire Origin** |
| 20% | **Edison** |
| 10% | **Golden Rooster** |
| 10% | **Lord Stan** |

- Rock General, Fire Origin, Edison and Golden Rooster are real Legend bosses (120,000–150,000 health, level 60) and each drops its card. With a random roll and only two summons per run, collecting all four cards will take more than one run.
- **Lord Stan** is a joke result. He has about 3,700 health and doesn't fight. He just says *"The floor is quite stiff. Maybe I went the wrong way... Still, I'm glad to see you, my friend. Goodbye."* and wanders off.

## The wooden door

When **Titehelm dies**, everyone sees **"You hear something wooden collapsing to the east..."** and a wooden barrier gives way. Kha-Anata arrives roughly 10–30 seconds later.

# The Palace

## Morale Killer
- **Start / hand-in:** Soumit (Middle Land). The Saithions revere their Patricians and Royal Priests; killing them is meant to shatter morale.
- **Objective:** Kill **5 Sailen Patricians** and **2 Sailen Royal Priests**.
- **Reward:** 33,000 XP and 3 gold 64 silver. No item.

## A Stray Warrior
- **Start:** **Sayiddy**, inside the Palace. **Hand in:** Soumit.
- **Story:** Sayiddy says he came to help the Daisauros and to find out what the Saithions are up to. *"Anyway, this isn't the best time or place for a long chat, so let me get to it..."*
- **Objective:** Escort Sayiddy to where he wants to go.
- **How it plays out:**
  - After the second boss (Crethiyum) dies, you get the message **"Do you hear strange noises from that well in the middle of the square?"** and **Sayiddy** shows up.
  - Choose **"I'm in for glory"** (Normal) or **"Yes, I'd be glad to help; you seem like a hero"** (Legend) and he starts walking.
  - When he reaches the **Shield of Primeval Serpent** he says **"It's my time to go... only our blood can break the seal. Keep moving forward and don't look back."** He then dies, which breaks the seal and completes the quest's objective. The last boss, **Zargis**, appears roughly 10–30 seconds afterwards.
  - Soumit's hand-in text acknowledges Sayiddy's death: *"But we should not let Sayiddy's death be in vain..."*
- **Reward:** 33,000 XP and 1 gold 82 silver. No item.
- It's unknown what happens if Sayiddy dies before he reaches the gate, so keep him alive until then.

### Sayiddy's Soul (spirit helper)
There is a ghostly **Sayiddy's Soul** you can speak to: *"My soul has been shackled here for an eternity... Choose the spirit ancestor that can help you the most in your battles."* Options: **Water Spirit Ancestor**, **Fire Spirit Ancestor** or **Earth Spirit Ancestor**, or *"Not now"*. Picking one summons that Spirit Ancestor for you.

### Signposts
- **Left Guidepost:** *"The left hides a lot of danger."*
- **Right Guidepost:** *"The right exposes you to a small number of elite monsters."*

## At The Gate
- **Start / hand-in:** Soumit. The Daisauros want to open the Saithions' Underworld gate so their enemies fight one another.
- **Objective:** Kill **Zargis**, the guardian. Normal and Legend Zargis both count.
- **Reward:** choose **Brilliant Magic Ring**, **Brilliant Fierce Ring**, **Brilliant Rapid Ring** or **Brilliant Mighty Ring**, plus 33,000 XP and 3 gold 64 silver.

## Wanted by Aryurumoka: Zargis (Daily)
- **Start / hand-in:** Arzarany. *"Zargis is no mere guard any longer; he has become the perfect warrior."*
- **Objective:** Kill Zargis. **Legend mode only.**
- **Reward:** **Essence of Soul Signet**, plus 24,000 XP and 4 gold 33 silver.

## A Call to Arms (Daily)
- **Start / hand-in:** Kristna. Sailen Royal Priests and their Hounds are heading deep into the Palace.
- **Objective:** Kill **4 Sailen Royal Priests** and **2 Sailen Hounds**. **Legend mode only.**
- **Reward:** **Core of Soul Signet**, plus 24,000 XP and 4 gold 33 silver.

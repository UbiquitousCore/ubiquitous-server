# Relic's Key — Quest & NPC Guide

*Player-style guide. Everything below is something you would see on screen: quest text, NPC dialogue, shouts, rewards. Money is converted to the coins you will see in game (1 gold = 100 silver = 10,000 copper).*

**Dungeon:** Relic's Key (the swamp eye) · minimum level 20 in Normal mode, 60 in Legend mode · up to 4 players
**Bosses:** **Hathraad**, **Kermode** and **Alr-Rahjil**, fought in that order. See the boss guide.

## I've Made a Huge Mistake

- **Start / hand-in:** Tharrath, the alchemist, in the swamp library in High Forest.
- **Suggested group size:** 4.
- **Prerequisites:** a short chain, **Permission to Enter** (Marucathran, level 25) → **Alchemist** (Tharrath, level 26: bring back the book *"The Last Eternal"* from Eoltarblod) → this quest.
- **Story:** Tharrath admits that the giant wyrm you summoned and killed was *"kind of sort of the Lord of the Swamp"*, the thing that was keeping the marauding demons away. Now the demons are building an altar to open a gateway to the Netherworld.
- **Objective:** Help Tharrath, kill **Alr-Rahjil** in Relic's Key. Normal and Legend Alr-Rahjil both count.
- **Hand-in text:** *"Balance has been restored. The Lord of Sunsaya will never truly die, of course. It will resurrect."*
- **Reward:** choose one of **Soul Gorger Knife**, **Soul Striker Axe** or **Soul Taker Bow**, plus 14,400 XP and 1 gold 23 silver.

## Wanted by Aryurumoka: Alr-Rahjil (Daily)

- **Start / hand-in:** Arzarany at the Aryurumoka camp (High Forest, southeast past Greenmont). The Eristars describe Alr-Rahjil as *"a powerful demon who fled Sinskaald"* and is now hiding in Relic's Key.
- **Objective:** Kill Alr-Rahjil. **Legend mode only.**
- **Reward:** **Essence of Soul Signet**, plus 24,000 XP and 4 gold 33 silver.

## The Ghosts Inside (Daily)

- **Start / hand-in:** Kristna at the Aryurumoka camp. The missive says the spirits of an infamous hunter and gladiator haunt Relic's Key.
- **Objective:** Kill **2 Ghostly Hunters** and **5 Ghostly Gladiators**. **Legend mode only.** These ghosts arrive in the dungeon's attack waves (see below).
- **Reward:** **Core of Soul Signet**, plus 24,000 XP and 4 gold 33 silver.

## Inside the dungeon: Tharrath's ritual

Relic's Key is not a fight-your-way-through dungeon. The whole run is built around protecting Tharrath while he works.

### 1. Walking to the altar

- You start at the entrance with a level-28 **Tharrath** (a friendly NPC with 8,000 health; 25,000 in Legend). Talk to him.
- He says: *"We've arrived. The altar is ahead. Are you ready?"* Choose **"Yes!"** (or "No, I'm not ready" to wait).
- He shouts **"Follow me!"** and walks to the altar by himself. Stay with him.
- On arrival the Tharrath you were following disappears and a second Tharrath takes the altar. He begins channelling a spell called **Summoning**, which shows as an aura on him for the whole ritual.

### 2. Defending him (three waves, one boss each)

Tharrath comments on what's coming. Use his shouts as your cue:

| Stage | Tharrath says | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Wave 1 starts | **"Here they come, right on time!"** (about 5 seconds after the ritual begins) | Sons of the Earth, Lake and Rotten Lake. A first group appears almost at once, more follow at about 35 s and 65 s. Once the key monsters are dead there is a ~20 s pause. |
| Boss 1 | **"The air is humming... Must be a giant one!"** | **Hathraad** appears. |
| Wave 2 | **"More of them are coming!"** | Ghostly Hunters, Gladiators, Mages and Priests arrive in three groups, at about 25 s, 55 s and 85 s. A further pause of ~20 s follows the last kill. |
| Boss 2 | **"An angry spirit approaches!"** | **Kermode** appears. |
| Wave 3 | **"You've got to hold them off!"** | The biggest wave. It mixes Sons, Ghostly units, Flame Strikers, Lava-Skin Burners and Rock-Wings Assassins, in five groups spaced out over roughly three minutes (first group about 20 s in, then about 50 s, 90 s, 135 s and 185 s). |
| Boss 3 | **"I'm almost finished!"** | **Alr-Rahjil** appears. |
| Done | **"It's done!"** | Roughly 10 seconds after the last boss falls, he says: *"Woo! That was rather exhausting. I need to wait here, recover my strength... Find me later in the Wyrgast's Library."* The dungeon ends. |

**If Tharrath dies**, the ritual does not appear able to continue, so treat him dying as a failed run.

### Legend mode differences in this event

- The event plays out the same way, but every enemy is a stronger Legend version (level 60 and much more health). The **Ghostly** units in wave 2 and wave 3 are what count for *The Ghosts Inside*.
- Legend runs also add the **exhaustion** mechanic. See the boss guide.
- Tharrath himself has 25,000 health instead of 8,000.

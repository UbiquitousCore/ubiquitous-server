# Knahswahs Prison — Quest & NPC Guide

*Player-style guide. Everything below is something you would see on screen: quest text, NPC dialogue, rewards, shouts, buff names. Money is converted to the coins you will see in game (1 gold = 100 silver = 10,000 copper).*

**Dungeon:** Knahswahs Prison (entered from High Forest) · minimum level 10 · up to 4 players
**Modes:** Normal and Legend. The three Prison bosses are **Ariel**, the **Executioner of Knahswahs** and **Cosmo**. See the boss guide for the fights.

---

## I Predict a Riot!

- **Start:** Bollen. **Hand in:** Lord Dugen.
- **Story:** Word has reached Bellshoal of a riot at Knahswahs Prison. Cosmo, a member of Darksong, is said to have incited it, but Bollen doesn't believe it: *"he's quiet, calm, collected."* You're sent to find out what's going on.
- **Objective:** Kill Cosmo.
- **Notes:** Cosmo only shows up after the other two bosses are dead, roughly 10 to 30 seconds after the second one falls. Killing the Normal Cosmo or the Legend Cosmo both count.
- **Hand-in text:** *"So Cosmo was consumed by a demon, eh?"*
- **Reward:** choose one of **Deported Wizard's Signet**, **Ring of Endless Torment** or **Circle of Cold Wind**, plus 5,200 XP and 82 silver.

## Flowers in Graveyard

- **Start / hand-in:** Deborah.
- **Story:** Her father, Anthony Benson, was a criminal who ended up in Knahswahs Prison. He regretted it and asked to be buried inside the prison walls. She visits his tomb every year on his birthday, but the riot stops her this year.
- **You receive:** *Deborah's flowers* when you accept the quest.
- **Objective:** Leave the flowers on the tombstone inside the prison. The quest log tracks a "Present flowers" step. The tombstone is deep inside the prison, on the far side from the entrance.
- **Reward:** 4,000 XP and 49 silver. No item.

## Overdue Loans

- **Start / hand-in:** Gideon Greenmill, the librarian.
- **Story:** Ariel, an old ranger turned prison guard, borrowed Gideon's only copy of *"Legends of Lut'Hiran"* and then died in the riots.
- **Objective:** Find the **Tattered Copy of "Legends of Lut'Hiran"** and bring it back.
- **Where it comes from:** It is a guaranteed (100%) drop from **Ariel**, in Normal or Legend.
- **Hand-in text:** Gideon is horrified by the state of the book (*"Covered in blood and filth"*).
- **Reward:** 4,000 XP and 49 silver. No item.

## Wanted by Aryurumoka: Cosmo (Daily)

- **Start / hand-in:** Arzarany (the Aryurumoka camp). The Eristars say they collect soul signets and hunt servants of evil. They describe Cosmo as *"a third-rate wizard, rejected by his peers"* who has become a cleric serving the demonic order.
- **Objective:** Kill Cosmo in Knahswahs Prison. The text says **you must complete this quest in Legend mode**, and only the Legend Cosmo counts.
- **Reward:** **Essence of Soul Signet**, plus 24,000 XP and 4 gold 33 silver.

## Prison Break (Daily)

- **Start / hand-in:** Kristna, who reads out a missive from "the people of Aryurumoka": two especially vicious convicts, a deranged orc and a cunning elf, are on the loose.
- **Objective:** Kill one **Vicious Orc Prisoner** and one **Vicious Elf Prisoner** in Knahswahs Prison. **Legend mode only.**
- **Reward:** **Core of Soul Signet**, plus 24,000 XP and 4 gold 33 silver.

## In the prison: NPCs and things to interact with

### Andare, the monk (the NPC you can talk to)

Andare is a level 10 friendly NPC standing inside the prison, a short way in from the entrance on the way toward the jail door. He has **no quest**. He is a buff-giver.

1. Talk to him. He says: *"Aha, it seems Shon no longer wishes for me to serve him. As a monk who has served the god of death, my skills may help you in your future battles."*
2. Choose **"What kind of skills?"**
3. He shouts **"It's a blessing from the Lord of the Dead!"** and casts **Holy Cloak** on you.
4. **Holy Cloak:** "Increase your shadow resistance by 150." It lasts **30 minutes**. This is useful against Cosmo's shadow damage.

While Human Prisoners are standing near him he occasionally shouts, alternating between *"I'll tell you again: the judgment on me is not justice!"* and *"Forget about it! I will never join you!"* (checked every 30 seconds).

### The jail door

When **both Ariel and the Executioner are dead**, everyone gets the system message **"You hear a faraway gate creaking open..."** and the jail door opens. **Cosmo** then appears somewhere between roughly 10 and 30 seconds later, so be ready for the last fight.
